About SafeBabyBath

Founded by parents, for parents — because bath time should be the best part of the day.

How It All Started

SafeBabyBath was born in 2022 from a very real problem — a first-time dad who dropped his soap while trying to shampoo his 9-month-old, causing a brief but terrifying slip.

That moment led to months of research, prototyping, and testing with pediatric safety experts to create the duck stand you see today — a product that keeps babies upright and parents in control.

Today, SafeBabyBath products are trusted by over 40,000 families across North America, and our mission remains the same: make bath time safe, simple, and genuinely joyful.
