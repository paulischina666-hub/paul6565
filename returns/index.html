Returns

We offer a 30-day return policy. You have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return.

Eligibility for Return

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you. If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item is defective or damaged.
